## Scope and applicability

Use this fictional procedure only for a P4800 hardware revision C that powers on normally but has a dark, flickering or dim lamp. It must not be used for revisions A or B. Do not infer the hardware revision from the serial format.

## Safety and symptom checks

Stop and transfer the case to the safety process for injury, smoke, scorching, unusual odour or excessive heat. Otherwise record whether the display stays on, whether the lamp fault affects every attempt, and whether a code is shown.

## Customer checks

### Step 1 — Cooling interval

Ask the customer to switch off, unplug the unit and allow the treatment head to cool for twenty minutes before inspecting any accessible part.

### Step 2 — External head and tray position

Once cool, ask the customer to reseat the external treatment head and push the filter tray fully home until its visible latch is level. Do not ask the customer to defeat an interlock or open the enclosure.

### Step 3 — Output window condition

Ask the customer to look for a removable obstruction or dry surface dust on the output window. Cleaning is limited to a dry lint-free cloth. Do not advise liquid cleaners or solvents.

## Disposition

Record the resolving check if output returns and remains stable. If the lamp stays dark, flickers again or a code remains, transfer the case to service and do not promise a replacement or return before human approval.
