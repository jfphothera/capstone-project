## Scope and applicability

Use this fictional procedure for a powered P3410 at revisions A, B or C when the customer reports a dark, intermittent or dim lamp. A dark power-supply indicator is a power problem and belongs to the P3410 power procedure instead.

## Safety and symptom checks

Stop troubleshooting and transfer the case for safety review if the customer reports injury, smoke, scorching, unusual odour or excessive heat. Otherwise confirm that the power-supply and unit indicators stay lit while the lamp fault occurs, and record any displayed code.

## Customer checks

### Step 1 — Cool and disconnect

Ask the customer to switch the unit off, unplug the external supply from the wall and allow the treatment head to cool for twenty minutes.

### Step 2 — External head connection

Once cool, ask the customer to reseat only the keyed external treatment-head connector until its collar is fully engaged. Do not advise forcing the connector, opening the head or touching its contacts.

### Step 3 — Clear output window

Ask the customer to check that the output window is unobstructed. Visible dust may be removed with a dry lint-free cloth. Do not recommend liquid cleaners or solvents.

## Disposition

Record a successful reseat or cleaning result. If the lamp remains dark or unstable, or if a code remains visible, transfer the case to service. A person must decide whether a part movement or return is appropriate.
