## Scope and prerequisites
This independently fictional user manual covers ordinary customer observations for Series-M revisions A, B and C. These exterior checks are authorized across all supported variants; they do not authorize a repair or revision-specific service procedure. Establish the model from the label or the fictional equipment record. Account verification is not required for these public instructions.
## Safety before operation
Stop using the device if smoke, an unusual burning smell, sparking, excessive heat or an injury is reported. Disconnect the unit if safe and contact support. Do not open the enclosure, remove a cover, bypass an interlock or change prescribed treatment settings.
## Basic power-on observations
With the unit idle and no safety concern present, confirm whether the accessible power lead is connected and whether the normal power indicator illuminates. Observe the controller display and bank indicator. If the display remains dark, record that observation and contact support for the exact equipment procedure; do not replace fuses or investigate internal wiring.
## Lamp-start observations
If the display powers on but the modular lamp bank stays dark, read the visible bank-ready indicator and any message on the display. Record whether all lamps or only one area is affected. Do not start an additional treatment as a diagnostic test. Share the observations so support can select the applicable controlled troubleshooting sequence.
## Indicators and user-permitted care
A displayed code identifies a condition for support to review; this fictional manual does not authorize guessing a code meaning or generating an unlock or refill code. Record the complete code. User care is limited to an exterior visual check while the unit is switched off and cool; do not remove parts, clean internal components or reset service settings.
## Next step and limits
If these observations do not establish the symptom, ask a clarifying question and retain what the customer already supplied. Revision-specific steps require a verified applicable document. Internal controller measurements, component replacement, returns, shipments and account changes follow separate procedures and approval controls.
