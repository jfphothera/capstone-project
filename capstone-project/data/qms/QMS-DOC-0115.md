## Scope and applicability

Use this fictional procedure for a powered Series-M at revisions A, B or C when one lamp, one bank or the whole lamp area is dark, flickering or visibly uneven. Do not use it for a completely unpowered unit.

## Safety and symptom checks

Stop troubleshooting and transfer the case for safety review if the customer reports injury, smoke, scorching, unusual odour or excessive heat. Otherwise record how many lamp positions are affected, whether the same positions fail each time, and whether the controller displays a code.

## Customer checks

### Step 1 — Cool and isolate

Ask the customer to switch the unit off, unplug it and wait thirty minutes. Lamp banks and nearby surfaces must be cool before any visual check.

### Step 2 — External airflow check

Ask the customer to confirm that no cloth, packaging or furniture blocks the external ventilation openings. They may remove loose external dust with a dry cloth. Do not ask them to remove a panel or guard.

### Step 3 — Visible obstruction check

From outside the closed guard, ask whether an object or shipping material is visibly blocking the lamp area. If anything is behind the guard, stop and transfer to service rather than instructing the customer to reach inside.

## Disposition

If clear airflow resolves an intermittent report, record the result. A lamp or bank that remains dark, uneven or associated with a code requires service review. Part shipment and return decisions remain human-authorized actions.
