## Preparing a return
Package the device securely in its original box. If that box is unavailable or damaged, ask support for a reviewed packaging arrangement before returning the device. Do not operate a damaged or safety-flagged unit to prepare it for return.
## Carrier pickup
When simulated pickup is arranged, keep the packaged device indoors and hand it directly to the simulated carrier at the arranged pickup. Do not leave it unattended outside. The arrangement allows up to three attempts; contact support if collection remains unsuccessful.
## Customer drop-off
For a drop-off return, attach the supplied simulated label to the securely packaged device and take it to a simulated carrier drop-off location. A created label does not establish carrier acceptance, pickup or delivery.
## Tracking and replacement
Tracking refers only to the recorded simulated shipment. Carrier delivery of a return is followed by a separate receiving check. Any replacement device is considered only after the original has been received and accepted, and still requires authorization. No real carrier is contacted by this demonstration.
