## Finding the identification label
The serial number is printed on the device identification label. Read the accessible label without opening the enclosure or removing a cover. If the label is unreadable, describe that to support; do not guess missing characters.
## Describing the problem
Tell support whether the device remains completely dark, whether its display turns on while its lamps stay off, or whether a message appears. Include the exact displayed words or code. These observations can be discussed before account verification.
## When to stop
If there is smoke, an unusual burning smell, sparking, excessive heat, or a reported injury, stop using the device and disconnect it if safe. Contact support for a safety review. Do not attempt repair or resume a treatment to test the symptom.
