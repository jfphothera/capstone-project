## Prepare the response
Use the current verified case facts and customer-disclosable sources. Keep restricted operational instructions out of customer prose. Acknowledge the actual request, ask only for missing information and distinguish an unknown revision from an already identified unit.
## Review and approval
Validate factual consistency, source applicability, disclosure and grounding before release. Retain rejected drafts and their reasons. Outgoing representative email requires named customer_email approval, including edited or regenerated drafts. Approval never substitutes for a valid document or factual consistency.
## Follow-up and closure
Preserve the full conversation on handoff. Explain the next authorized action without a delivery or resolution promise. Record follow-up observations, outcomes and the representative's resolution or unresolved closure. Improvement suggestions are reviewed separately and cannot amend controlled procedures automatically.
