## Scope and applicability

This procedure applies to the P2400 portable phototherapy unit at all hardware
revisions. Use it when the customer reports the unit shows no signs of power
at all.

The P2400 is the only portable unit in the range with an internal battery, so
a unit that appears dead may simply be flat. That possibility is checked first
because it resolves most reports on this model.

## Symptom confirmation

Ask whether the charge indicator shows anything at all when the mains adapter
is connected, even briefly. A single amber flash on connection means the
battery is deeply discharged rather than the unit being dead.

Ask whether the unit was last used on battery or on mains.

## Step 1 — Charge the battery

Ask the customer to connect the mains adapter and leave the unit on charge for
a full two hours without attempting to switch it on. A deeply discharged P2400
will not respond to the power button until the battery passes a minimum
threshold, which looks exactly like a dead unit.

Ask them to confirm the adapter indicator is lit, which shows the adapter
itself is working.

## Step 2 — Adapter and lead

If the adapter indicator is dark, ask the customer to try the adapter in a
different outlet confirmed live with a known-working lamp or appliance. Do not
ask the customer to test any outlet with a metallic probe or tool.

The P2400 adapter lead is a customer-replaceable accessory if it is visibly
damaged, kinked or the moulding has separated from the plug body.

## Step 3 — Battery age

Check the ERP record for the install date. P2400 batteries are a consumable
and a unit several years old that no longer holds charge needs a battery
service rather than troubleshooting.

## Step 4 — Disposition

If the unit starts after charging, record the resolution and advise the
customer about charge habits. If it stays dark with a working adapter after a
full two-hour charge, raise a service request referencing this procedure ID
and the unit serial number. Do not instruct the customer to open the
enclosure or attempt to change the battery themselves.
