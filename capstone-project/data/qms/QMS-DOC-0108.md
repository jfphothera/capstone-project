## Scope and applicability

This procedure applies to the 3-Series cabinet unit at all hardware revisions
when the unit powers on normally but one or more lamps fail to light, or output
is reported as weak. The unit must be powering on; if it is completely dead,
use the no-power procedure for this model instead.

## Symptom confirmation

Establish how many lamps are affected and whether the same lamp fails every
time. The 3-Series carries several lamps in a bank, and a single failed lamp
presents very differently from a whole bank that will not strike.

Ask the customer whether the panel shows a lamp fault code. A single lamp
reported as dark with no fault code is usually an end-of-life lamp; a whole
bank with a fault code is not.

## Step 1 — Lamp hours

Ask the customer to read the lamp hour counter from the service screen. Lamps
at or beyond their rated service interval are a consumable replacement, and no
further troubleshooting is required before fitting a new one.

Lamp modules are a customer-replaceable consumable on the 3-Series. Confirm the
customer has the replacement in hand before asking them to remove the old one.

## Step 2 — Lamp seating

Ask the customer to switch the unit off, unplug it, and allow the bank to cool
for at least twenty minutes before touching anything. Once cool, ask them to
check that each lamp is seated squarely in its holders at both ends.

A lamp that has worked loose in transport will not strike and looks identical
to a failed lamp from the front.

## Step 3 — Reflector condition

Ask the customer to look for dust or film on the reflector behind the lamps.
Advise cleaning with a dry lint-free cloth only. Do not advise solvents,
alcohol wipes or any liquid cleaner, which damage the reflector coating and
void the warranty on the bank assembly.

## Step 4 — Disposition

If a replacement lamp or reseating restores output, record the resolution. If a
whole bank will not strike with lamps in good condition, raise a service
request referencing this procedure ID and the unit serial number.
