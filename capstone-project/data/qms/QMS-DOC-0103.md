## Scope and applicability

This revision 1.0 procedure covered the P4800 revision A unit when it shipped with
the original mains inlet assembly. It was withdrawn on 14 March 2025 and replaced by
QMS-DOC-0101 version 3.0.

This document is retained for audit traceability only. It must not be used to
generate customer-facing troubleshooting instructions. It is present in the
retrieval corpus deliberately, so that the validation layer can be shown rejecting a
semantically relevant but withdrawn source.

## Symptom confirmation

Confirm with the customer that the unit shows no status LED, no fan noise, and no
display backlight when the mains switch is set to the on position.

## Step 1 — Mains supply check

Ask the customer to reseat the mains cord at both ends and confirm the wall outlet
is live using a known-working appliance.

## Step 2 — Fuse inspection

With the mains cord disconnected, ask the customer to slide out the rear fuse
carrier and inspect both fuse elements.

Note: the fuse rating quoted in this withdrawn version was corrected in version 3.0
following a component change. This is the principal reason the document was
superseded and is why it must not be served to customers.

## Step 3 — Disposition

If the unit remains dark, raise a return authorisation.
