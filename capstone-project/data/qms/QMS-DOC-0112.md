## Scope and applicability

Use this fictional procedure for a P2400 at revisions A, B or C when the unit powers on but the lamp does not start, flickers, or appears unusually dim. Do not use it for a completely unpowered unit or for any report of smoke, injury, scorching, unusual odour or excessive heat.

## Safety and symptom checks

Stop and transfer the case to the safety process if a hazard or injury is reported. Otherwise confirm that the display remains on, record whether the lamp is dark, intermittent or dim, and ask whether a fault code is visible.

## Customer checks

### Step 1 — Complete cooling interval

Ask the customer to switch the unit off, disconnect the power lead and let the lamp housing cool for twenty minutes. No lamp or cover should be touched while warm.

### Step 2 — Treatment program state

After reconnecting, ask the customer to confirm that a treatment program is started rather than paused and that the timer is counting down. If the timer is not running, record the display state and stop this procedure.

### Step 3 — Accessible lamp-module seating

With the unit off, unplugged and cool, the customer may press the external lamp module until its two visible latches are level. Do not ask the customer to open the enclosure, remove a guard or touch electrical contacts.

## Disposition

If the lamp starts and remains stable, record which check resolved the report. If it remains dark, flickers again or shows a fault code, arrange human service review and do not authorize a replacement or return automatically.
